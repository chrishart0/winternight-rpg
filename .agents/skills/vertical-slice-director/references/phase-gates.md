# Phase-gate contract

For every gate, record the current scope, required deliverables, exact commands and observable
results, unresolved risks, the remain/advance decision, and one bounded next action. A file's
existence or a successful serializer call is never sufficient evidence.

## Phase 0 — pinned-engine integration

A clean checkout must bootstrap, compile the legally clean minimal project, load it through the
pinned engine and editor, execute start/end dialogue and victory, pass reference and determinism
checks, and document an interactive Linux launch. Campaign content and generated art remain out of
scope.

## Phase 1 — schemas and compiler

Structured models, exported schemas, validation, the LT adapter, events, maps, manifest, and golden
or semantic-determinism fixtures must regenerate the minimal project without hand-editing engine
files. Evidence must bind the exact engine commit, schema version, content hash, and project tree.

## Phase 2 — complete graybox campaign

All four connected chapters must be playable from title to ending with legally clean placeholders,
two reused layouts/four narrative variants, final objective structures, intro/outro and win/loss
paths, and no AI art. Require pinned-runtime loading, objective truth tables, a real-input completion
route, and visual inspection of native engine frames. Forced-event traversal is supporting evidence,
not a substitute for the input route.

## Phase 3 — narrative content

Every mission and scene must reference valid beat IDs and carry the correct adaptation status.
Dialogue must be original paraphrase, the inferred village sequence must be explicit, all named
setup characters and chapter transitions must be understandable, and validation must reject the
Chapter 6 boundary. Require runtime execution of every scene plus a narrative review against the
canon and adaptation ledgers.

## Phase 4 — visual assets

Generate only after Phase 2 is recorded as passed. Every approved source must follow the visual
bible and record prompt, references, provider/model, seed when supported, hashes, processing
version, approval, and provenance. Deterministic processing must pass LT dimensions, sheet regions,
palette/color-key, and reference checks. Require fresh hash-bound screenshots and actual vision
inspection for identity, clipping, text fit, chroma defects, and location-state continuity.

## Phase 5 — balance and packaging

Require a deterministic private package, isolated packaged-launch smoke, save/continue and Game
Over recovery, three complete post-balance human playthroughs, 45–75 minute totals, and recorded
difficulty, tutorial/objective clarity, display, music, and SFX judgments. Automated decode,
pathfinding, and completion cannot close subjective human gates. Any balance or clarity change
restarts the three-run count.

## Phase 6 — generator extraction

Only after the slice passes Phase 5, move story assumptions out of compiler infrastructure, refine
skills from observed failure cases, and compile a second unrelated content pack without changing
the compiler. Require deterministic output and pinned-runtime initialization for both packs. A
portable `.agents/skills` layout proves discoverability; actual Hermes execution is separate
cross-tool evidence when Hermes is available.

## Evidence lessons

- Bind every runtime artifact to the current project-tree and build-manifest hashes; reject stale
  evidence instead of carrying it forward after compilation.
- Distinguish static analysis, forced event execution, public-trigger mechanics, real-input engine
  traversal, real-display launch, packaged launch, and human playtesting in every claim.
- Preserve flow captures when refreshing a screenshot gallery, and inspect the actual pixels rather
  than treating image creation as visual success.
- Decode/start audio in the pinned runtime and verify deterministic provenance, but leave loudness,
  loop seams, fatigue, and thematic resemblance to human listening.
- Never refresh the root build while the editor is open. Avoid replacing it under a running player;
  use a detached checkout for authoritative verification until the player closes.
