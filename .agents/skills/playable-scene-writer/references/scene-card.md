# Scene card

Fill this before rewriting a scene. Do not paste it into YAML.

| Field | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Who | Relationship words a first-time player can use (father, friend, known peddler, the two strangers). |
| Where | Matches the background or the map the player will see after the talk. Arrival talk must not describe an empty road if the next board is the village. |
| Want | One present-tense desire (deliver cider, hang the Green, hide, fetch cloth). |
| Turn | The last line leaves the situation different from the first. |
| Play | The next public action the game actually exposes. |

## Line jobs

A line should carry character and information together. Cut lore that is not doing work in this scene. Festival, home, and friendship come before banners, titles, and distant wars.

## Recurring line types

- **Handoff:** names the next person, place, or object the player can identify on the map.
- **Pickup:** care, fear, or memory — not "I obtained X."
- **Combat quote:** the speaker under pressure. Players hear these more than cutscenes.
- **Rescue / civilian:** one human detail, then the next tactical need.
- **Tutorial narration:** keep required menu words (`Item`, `Equip`) in narration, after a story beat, not instead of one.
