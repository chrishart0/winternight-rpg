# Winternight story pass

Authored narrative layer for the four-chapter slice. Dialogue is original paraphrase.
The playable arc ends when Rand rejoins wounded Tam with supplies. Tam's fever speech,
parentage, and birthplace remain outside the boundary.

## Throughline

1. Ordinary cart, festival work, and friendship, with a first unconfirmed rider.
2. News and strangers stain the Green without yet breaking it.
3. Home is quiet, then the door goes. Tam's skill saves Rand. Tam comes back wounded.
4. Inferred: the same night spends the village. The inn holds. Wider roofs do not.
5. Rand walks back alone, searches the ruin as home, and faces one leftover Trolloc.
6. He returns with water, cloth, a blanket, and the unexplained sword. They turn toward
   Emond's Field. The game fades.

## Voices

Rand stays frightened and untested. Tam is spare, competent, unexplained. Mat jokes.
Perrin notices. Egwene organizes. Fain sells unease. Thom measures faces. Moiraine
spends little and answers less. Lan holds doors.

Named friends do not appear on the Chapter 2 map. Their absence is inferred, not a
death claim.

## Adaptation marks

`inferred` or `gameplay_invention`. Direct beats keep to the authorized notes.
